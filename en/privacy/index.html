D-METER — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

D-METER (“the app”) is a fuel density calculator for iOS developed by Pavlo Kokaryev (“we”, “us”). This policy describes what information the app handles and how.

Summary

  • We do not collect personal data through the app.
  • The app has no account or sign-in.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party crash-reporting software.
  • The app does not send your data to us or to any third-party server. It communicates only with Apple’s App Store, through Apple’s StoreKit, for the subscription.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps a few settings on your device so that it opens the way you left it: the interface language, the theme, whether the Blending demo button is shown, and whether the introductory tutorial has been completed. These settings stay on your device, are not sent to us, and are removed when you delete the app.

Values you enter for calculations are processed on your device. The app does not save them after it is closed.

Purchases and subscriptions

D-METER PRO Monthly is an auto-renewable subscription sold through the Apple App Store. Payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details, Apple ID or billing address.

To decide whether PRO features are available, the app asks Apple’s StoreKit framework on your device for the status of your subscription. The transaction information StoreKit returns is used on the device only for this purpose, is kept in memory only, and is not sent to us.

Apple provides developers with sales and subscription reports in App Store Connect. Apple’s handling of your purchase information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Diagnostics provided by Apple

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with crash reports and aggregated usage statistics. You control this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. Such data is provided by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Web pages opened from the app

The app opens this policy, the Terms of Use and the Support page in your web browser. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit them, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The app also opens Apple’s subscription management, which is governed by Apple.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use them only to reply to you and do not share them with anyone. You can send a request to delete your personal data to the address below. We consider such requests and delete data in accordance with applicable law and any retention obligations that apply to us.

Your choices

Because the app does not collect personal data, we hold no data about you from the app. You can remove all settings stored by the app by deleting it from your device. For anything about email correspondence, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the date above.
