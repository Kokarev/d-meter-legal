D-METER — Support

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Questions, problems or suggestions about D-METER: write to almasoft.team@gmail.com.

To help us answer quickly, please include your device model, iOS version, the app version (Settings → APP → Version in D-METER), what you did and what happened, and a screenshot if possible.

Subscription

What does PRO include?
The Density Chart (virtual hydrometer), Blending and Density Escalation. The base calculator, including AIR details, is free.
How much does it cost? Is there a free trial?
The price and the availability of a free trial are determined by Apple StoreKit for your account and region, and are shown on the subscription screen before you confirm.
I already subscribed, but PRO is locked.
Open D-METER PRO from the app menu and tap “Restore purchases”. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID that was used for the purchase.
How do I cancel?
Tap “Manage subscription” in the app, or follow Apple’s instructions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal.
How do I get a refund?
Payments are processed by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at https://reportaproblem.apple.com/.
