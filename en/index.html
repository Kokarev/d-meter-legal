Petroleum cargo calculator for iPhone

Density, temperature, mass and volume — referred to 15 °C.

D-METER turns a density at 15 °C and an observed temperature into the numbers a cargo needs: density at temperature, mass and volume, with VAC and AIR values side by side.

One calculator, three working tools.
